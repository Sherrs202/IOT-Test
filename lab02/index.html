Lab 02 · Interface redesign

Better playlists, less searching.

My proposal for improving Spotify playlists. The idea is to make finding, sorting and organizing music easier, especially when a playlist contains many songs.

My redesign proposal

01 · The problem

Large playlists can become difficult to organize. Finding a song or choosing the right music for a mood may take too long.

02 · My solution

Add a clear search bar, mood filters and a simple way to preview and select songs before adding them to a playlist.

03 · The benefit

Users could spend less time managing their music and more time listening to songs they enjoy.

Interactive prototype

This is a student concept. It does not connect to or change your Spotify account.

Expected impact

My redesign focuses on a more organized and personalized playlist experience. Search and filters help users find songs faster, while the playlist builder makes it easier to review a selection before saving it.
