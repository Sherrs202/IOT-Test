My Favorites

My three favorite songs by Joji

Home About Works Favorites

1. Slow Dancing in the Dark

I chose this song because I really like its atmosphere and the combination of Joji's voice with the instrumental. It was also one of the songs that made me interested in his music.

2. Glimpse of Us

I chose this song because of its simple piano sound and emotional vocals. I think it is one of Joji's most memorable songs.

3. Sanctuary

I chose Sanctuary because I like its production and different atmosphere. It has a sound that makes it stand out from some of his slower songs.
