Biography

Joji, whose real name is George Kusunoki Miller, is a Japanese-Australian singer, songwriter, and music producer. He was born in Osaka, Japan. His music is known for combining alternative R&B, pop, electronic music, and lo-fi influences.

Important Facts

  • Full name: George Kusunoki Miller
  • Artist name: Joji
  • Born in Osaka, Japan
  • Occupation: Singer, songwriter, and producer
  • Musical styles include alternative R&B and pop

Career Highlights

Joji developed an international music career with releases such as BALLADS 1, Nectar, and SMITHEREENS. Songs including "Slow Dancing in the Dark", "Sanctuary", and "Glimpse of Us" became some of his best-known works.
